Omarchy Drive Sync / Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy describes how Miguel Cordero Collar’s personal, locally run rclone setup uses Google Drive data.

Effective and last updated: 8 November 2026

Who operates it

Omarchy Drive Sync is a personal application operated by Miguel Cordero Collar for his own Google Drive account. It runs on his Linux computer. The website is not a hosted sync service and does not provide visitor accounts or Drive access.

Data and permission

The app uses Google OAuth and the Drive API. Its broad Drive permission can technically allow reading, creating, changing, or deleting files across the account. The configured rclone job targets only one selected personal folder; this folder selection does not limit the permission Google grants.

During a sync, file contents and related metadata transfer between the owner’s Linux computer and Google Drive. The rclone configuration and OAuth token remain on that computer, outside this website and its repository. Private local operational logs may contain filenames.

Use and sharing

The sync workflow does not upload Drive files, tokens, or operational logs to this website. These pages do not include advertising or analytics. The workflow does not sell Google user data, use it for AI training, or send it to other third-party processors. Google processes Drive data to provide its service under Google’s own terms and policies.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Retention and backups

The owner controls retention of local operational logs and rollback copies. The setup also keeps separate backups on an external hard drive. Revoking Google access or stopping the scheduled job does not automatically delete existing copies from Google Drive, the Linux computer, or the external drive.

Your controls

The application is used only by its owner; no visitor data is collected through a sign-in flow. The owner can stop future automatic sync runs by stopping the local scheduled job, or revoke Google access from Google Account connections.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the public contact address listed on Miguel Cordero Collar’s website.
