# People

Public figures whose work has shaped how Miguel Cordero Collar thinks and works. His family and friends come first; this is a collection of public influences, not an endorsement of everything they have said or done.

Updated: 2026-10-11

## David Heinemeier Hansson

**Programmer, entrepreneur and racing driver** · Technology

His example makes ambitious work feel possible. He created Ruby on Rails, helped build 37signals and later created Omarchy, all while speaking plainly about his convictions. I admire the range of his life: software, business, writing, racing, politics and family all seem to receive serious attention.

David Heinemeier Hansson is a Danish programmer, author and co-owner of 37signals. He created Ruby on Rails, writes about software and public life, and races cars.

Links:
- [Personal website](https://dhh.dk/)
- [37signals](https://37signals.com/)
- [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/)

## George Hotz

**Hacker, programmer and entrepreneur** · Technology

George Hotz makes me want to act on ambitious ideas. He says what he thinks and is willing to challenge established companies. He builds in public, shares his reasoning and brings a playful energy to hard problems. His example encourages me to start before I have every answer and to show the work as it develops.

George Hotz, known as geohot, is an American hacker, programmer and entrepreneur. He became known for unlocking the original iPhone and hacking the PlayStation 3, and later founded comma.ai, the company behind the open-source driver-assistance software openpilot.

Links:
- [Personal website](https://geohot.github.io/)
- [openpilot](https://github.com/commaai/openpilot)
- [tinygrad](https://github.com/tinygrad/tinygrad)

## Elon Musk

**Entrepreneur, engineer and builder** · Technology

His companies taught me to think on a larger timescale and to take seemingly unrealistic technical ambitions seriously. I admire the willingness to question inherited assumptions, reduce a problem to its physical and economic constraints and attempt a solution. That influence remains real even when I disagree with his conduct or conclusions.

Elon Musk is an entrepreneur and engineer whose companies work across spaceflight, electric vehicles, artificial intelligence and infrastructure. He is known for pairing long-horizon ambitions with an insistence on first-principles thinking.

Links:
- [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/)
- [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/)
- [xAI](https://x.ai/)

## Miguel Anxo Bastos

**Professor of political science and economist** · Philosophy

His lectures introduced me to libertarianism and anarcho-capitalism. I admire his manner as much as his ideas: he argues with humility, courtesy and a real effort to understand the person in front of him. Watching him disagree without hostility made me more hopeful about human nature.

Miguel Anxo Bastos Boubeta is a Spanish economist and professor of political science at the University of Santiago de Compostela. His work covers Austrian economics, public policy and political philosophy.

Links:
- [Personal website](https://miguelanxobastos.org/)

## Ayn Rand

**Novelist and philosopher** · Philosophy

She showed me that a novel can carry an entire philosophy. Atlas Shrugged had a lasting effect on me because it made Objectivism vivid and memorable, even where I later came to question parts of it. I still admire the scale and confidence of what she attempted.

Ayn Rand was a Russian-American novelist and philosopher, best known for Atlas Shrugged and The Fountainhead. She developed Objectivism, a philosophy centered on reason, individual rights and rational self-interest.

## George Orwell

**Novelist, essayist and political writer** · Philosophy

Nineteen Eighty-Four was one of the books that brought me back to reading in my twenties. Together with Animal Farm, it sharpened my awareness of authoritarianism, propaganda and concentrated power. Orwell showed me that a story can make a political danger felt more powerfully than an abstract argument can.

George Orwell, the pen name of Eric Arthur Blair, was an English novelist, essayist and journalist. His political fiction, especially Nineteen Eighty-Four and Animal Farm, made authoritarianism, propaganda and the abuse of language vivid to generations of readers.

Links:

## Marcus Aurelius

**Roman emperor and Stoic philosopher** · Philosophy

Reading Meditations showed me how little our basic concerns have changed. Friendship, family, love, duty and the question of how to live well survive every technological and historical transformation. His notes were written for himself, which may be why reading one at a time still feels so direct.

Marcus Aurelius was a Roman emperor and Stoic philosopher. Meditations, his private reflections on character, duty and resilience, remains one of the clearest surviving records of Stoic practice.

- [Meditations at MIT Classics](http://classics.mit.edu/Antoninus/meditations.html)
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/marcus-aurelius/)

## Sam Harris

**Philosopher, neuroscientist and author** · Philosophy

Following Sam Harris during the New Atheism years pushed me to question claims more carefully and ask what evidence supports them. I admire the precision with which he speaks and his treatment of meditation as something that can be examined without abandoning a scientific outlook. Through his work, I found a practice that helps me observe my thoughts and regain perspective.

Sam Harris is an American philosopher, neuroscientist, author and host of the Making Sense podcast. His work spans rationality, ethics, consciousness, meditation and public debate, and he created the Waking Up meditation app.

Links:
- [Official website](https://www.samharris.org/)
- [Waking Up](https://www.wakingup.com/)
- [Making Sense podcast](https://www.samharris.org/podcasts/making-sense-episodes)

## Richard Dawkins

**Evolutionary biologist and author** · Philosophy

The Selfish Gene showed me that rigorous scientific ideas can also be clear and memorable. I admire Dawkins's contribution to the public understanding of evolution and the intellectual courage of his earlier writing on religion. I no longer agree with everything he says, but that does not erase what I learned from him.

Richard Dawkins is a British evolutionary biologist and author. The Selfish Gene and his wider popular-science writing helped bring gene-centred evolution and scientific reasoning to a broad audience.

## Christopher Hitchens

**Author, critic and debater** · Philosophy

His debates showed me what a quick, well-read mind can do in public. He combined wit with an unusual ability to find the heart of an argument in the moment. I do not take rhetorical confidence as proof that someone is right, but Hitchens taught me how much clarity and courage matter when speaking publicly.

Christopher Hitchens was an English-American author, columnist and literary critic whose writing ranged from politics and culture to religion. He was celebrated for his erudition, polemical wit and formidable debating style.

## Jordan B. Peterson

**Psychologist, author and lecturer** · Philosophy

His lectures gave me a language for thinking about stories, archetypes, responsibility and human nature at a time when those questions mattered to me. Maps of Meaning was difficult but rewarding, while 12 Rules for Life translated many of its ideas into everyday conduct. I now find some of his claims unconvincing, but his earlier work still influenced how I think about responsibility and meaning.

Jordan B. Peterson is a Canadian psychologist, author and online educator. His work connects psychology, mythology, religion and personal responsibility, from the academic Maps of Meaning to the practical advice of 12 Rules for Life.

## Thomas Sowell

**Economist, author and social theorist** · Economics

Thomas Sowell sharpened my respect for intellectual honesty. I value his clarity about economics and human behaviour, and his willingness to challenge popular ideas with evidence. His work encourages me to examine incentives and consequences, test my own assumptions and take an uncomfortable conclusion seriously.

Thomas Sowell is an American economist, author and social theorist whose work covers economics, history, culture and public policy. His books include Basic Economics, Knowledge and Decisions and A Conflict of Visions. He is a senior fellow emeritus at the Hoover Institution.

## Lee Kuan Yew

**Singapore's founding prime minister** · Economics

Lee Kuan Yew made me expect more from public leadership. I admire his sense of duty, his determination to unite a diverse society and his willingness to make difficult decisions for its future prosperity. He reminds me that caring about people requires competence, persistence and the courage to act when the necessary choice is unpopular.

Lee Kuan Yew was Singapore's first prime minister, serving from 1959 to 1990. With his cabinet, he helped shape the country's economic development and a shared national identity across ethnic and religious communities. His legacy also includes restrictions on political opposition and expression.

## Milton Friedman

**Economist, author and public intellectual** · Economics

Friedman changed how I think about incentives, monetary policy and the relationship between economic and individual freedom. I admire his ability to explain difficult ideas in plain language and defend them under pressure. I do not need to accept every conclusion he reached to recognize how deeply his work shaped my thinking.

Milton Friedman was an American economist, author and public intellectual who received the 1976 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences. His research and broadcasting helped bring monetary economics and the case for economic freedom to a broad audience.

## Antonio Escohotado

**Philosopher, jurist and essayist** · Philosophy

Antonio Escohotado taught me that independent thinking means being willing to change your mind. His books Historia general de las drogas and Los enemigos del comercio made me question inherited ideas about prohibition, property and freedom. I admire how he treated thinking, sharing and family as parts of the same full life.

Antonio Escohotado was a Spanish philosopher, jurist, sociologist and essayist. He taught Philosophy of Law and Sociology at the UNED and wrote on drugs, freedom, property and political ideas.
