## Most notes need very little

Most writing needs only paragraphs, a heading or two, and the occasional list. A plain `.md` file keeps the source readable on its own, and its Markdown is published beside the page.

- Write the point first.
- Add the example that makes it concrete.
- Stop when the idea is complete.
