Omarchy Drive Sync.

A private rclone setup that synchronizes one selected folder between Miguel Cordero Collar’s Linux computer and his own Google Drive account.

Owner: Miguel Cordero Collar · Updated 8 November 2026

What this app does

Omarchy Drive Sync is used by its owner for personal file synchronization. It runs locally on the owner’s computer. This website provides information only: visitors cannot sign in, connect a Drive account, upload files, or use a hosted sync service.

The app is independently operated by Miguel Cordero Collar and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Google Drive access

The OAuth permission used by the app is broad: technically, it can allow the app to read, create, change, or delete files across Google Drive. The configured sync job is limited to one selected personal folder. That configuration does not narrow the technical permission granted by Google.

File contents and related metadata transfer between the owner’s Linux computer and Google Drive during synchronization. They are not uploaded to this website.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Local storage and backups

The rclone configuration and OAuth token are kept on the owner’s computer, outside this website and its repository. Private local operational logs may contain filenames.

The setup keeps local rollback copies and separate backups on an external hard drive. The owner controls how long local logs and backup copies are retained. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete files or backups already present in Drive, on the computer, or on the external drive.

Stopping or revoking access

Stopping the owner’s scheduled job prevents future automatic sync runs. Google access can be revoked from the owner’s Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access after Google applies it; it does not remove existing copies.

Google processes Drive data as part of providing Google Drive and applies its own terms and policies. This sync workflow does not send data to advertising, analytics, AI-training systems, or other third-party processors.
