## Omarchy

**Category:** Technology
**Personal marker:** Started caring, July 2025
**Query:** Omarchy · 2025-01-01 2026-09-05 · Worldwide

[Open this Google Trends query](https://trends.google.com/explore?q=Omarchy&date=2025-01-01%202026-09-05&geo=Worldwide)

### What it is

Omarchy is a Linux distribution with a preconfigured desktop and a strong set of defaults. It is built for people who want to use, understand, and customize their computer.

### Link

- [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/)

### Personal context

### The Windows-to-Linux jump

For years I was tired of Windows performance issues and interested in Linux, but I kept postponing the switch. Gaming was part of the reason: when I still played games such as League of Legends, Linux felt like a compromise.

After some laptop problems, and once gaming mattered less, I installed Linux to see if it could help. In July 2025, while hiking in Asturias, I watched DHH’s interview with Lex Fridman. The ambition and positivity behind Omarchy clicked immediately.

It changed how I use my computer. I love understanding how computers work, customizing them, and treating the machine as something to explore. The idea of a computer for people who love computers felt like a description of me.

## AI

**Personal marker:** Discovered, Nov 2021
**Query:** AI · today 5-y · Worldwide

### What it is

Artificial intelligence is the field of building systems that perform tasks linked with human intelligence, such as learning, perception, language, and reasoning. This chart follows the broad search term AI, not one product.

### Link

- [Artificial intelligence](https://www.britannica.com/technology/artificial-intelligence)

### Personal context

### From autocomplete to ChatGPT

I was always interested in coding, but I started with the normal tools: Stack Overflow, documentation, and a lot of trial and error.

In November 2021, while writing my master’s thesis, an early GPT-style autocomplete tool appeared. It was not a chat interface yet, but the idea of software completing code felt important.

ChatGPT in November 2022 was the real break. I became obsessed with asking it questions, using it at work, exploring coding tools such as [v0](https://v0.dev), and talking about it with everyone.

I kept watching the conversation move through YouTube and Hacker News. The shift felt different from the skepticism around crypto: people I respected began treating AI as a real change in how software and knowledge work could happen.

### From curiosity to conviction

I was aware of Tesla around 2013, when friends and I talked about the Model S, electric cars, and what the future of transport might look like. I was interested in the engineering before I became interested in the stock.

That interest eventually became my master’s thesis: [Tesla’s Robotaxi network](/documents/Tesla%20Robotaxi%20-%20Master%20Thesis.pdf), a quantitative analysis of adoption, economic impact, and implications.
